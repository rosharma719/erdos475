# Reversal / tail reversal V ↦ z−V as a repair move for bad FP cycles

Tags: LSA1 FP avoidance balance DFL merge-chain PATH-AVOID free-point Avoid

Reversal maps point set V to z−V. Since a1+a2=z this swaps the conditions 'a1∉V' and 'a2∉V', so a bad cycle (containing both) maps to a bad cycle. Found independently in both approaches.

Do not repeat unless the specific failure is addressed.
